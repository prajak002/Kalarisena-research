"|action|" is the L2 norm of the residual action the active policy is issuing that step - a proxy for how hard it's pushing to correct, not a guarantee it's pushing the right way. It reads blank during RECOVERY, which drives the sim directly rather than through the tracker's residual-action interface. The compass arrow is the real vector from the capture point to the center of the support polygon - literally what capture-point margin is measured against, not a model's guess - and disappears once both feet lose contact, since there's no base of support left to define a "back inside" direction.
